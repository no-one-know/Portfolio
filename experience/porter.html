01 · SDE-2 ARCHITECTURAL LEADERSHIP

Zero-Downtime K2 to Spring Boot Migration & Domain-Driven Segregation

Porter's dynamic pricing and promotional campaigns originally lived inside a legacy Ktor (K2) service backed by a single centralized monolith database containing 18 entangled tables. Maintaining these tables and understanding what rules applied across which table had become a developer nightmare with severe regression risks. As SDE-2, I led the end-to-end architectural segregation: defining Domain-Driven Design (DDD) bounded contexts, carving out an independent Spring Boot service with its own dedicated database, and migrating mission-critical P0 endpoints (~70 RPS and ~27 RPS) with zero downtime through fast dark shadowing, Datadog metric diff mitigation, and a phased rollout.

The Legacy Bottleneck

18 Monolith DB Tables & Rule Maintenance Chaos

  • 18-Table Web: Pricing campaigns, partner promotions, vehicle slabs, and city surcharges were scattered across 18 unindexed, interconnected tables in the shared monolith DB.
  • Opaque Evaluation: It was nearly impossible to determine which campaign or rule was getting evaluated or taking precedence across which table, leading to severe cognitive overhead and high regression risk.
  • Shared Blast Radius: Complex multi-table joins on the monolith database during peak booking hours threatened relational lock contention across the entire trip booking lifecycle.
Architectural Strategy

DDD Bounded Contexts & Dedicated Service Database

  • Domain-Driven Segregation: Extracted the pricing campaign and fare calculation domain out of the monolithic Ktor (K2) codebase into an independent Spring Boot service written in Kotlin.
  • Storage Isolation: Provisioned a dedicated service database, completely severing operational dependence on the monolith DB.
  • Zero-Loss Data Migration: Engineered a comprehensive data migration pipeline that extracted, normalized, and reconciled historical campaign configurations from the 18 monolith tables into the new service DB.
High-Throughput P0 Endpoints

Mission-Critical Revenue & Settlement Paths

  • Pickup/Drop Pricing Estimation (~70 RPS): The primary landing screen API invoked whenever a customer selects pickup and drop locations. Must calculate real-time vehicle rates, dynamic surges, and active campaign discounts under heavy concurrency.
  • End-Trip Fare & Breach Settlement (~27 RPS): Triggered at the exact moment a driver completes a trip. Evaluates actual distance traveled, waiting times, toll charges, SLA/route breaches, and final ledger settlement with strict financial precision.
  • Both endpoints demanded sub-50ms p99 latency SLAs without a single dropped customer booking or driver payout session.
Validation & Rollout

Fast Shadowing, Datadog Diff Mitigation & Canary Rollout

  • Fast Dark Shadowing: Duplicated 100% of live incoming production requests asynchronously to the new Spring Boot service without adding latency to the primary user path.
  • Datadog Metrics & Diff Investigation: Instrumented Datadog dashboards to compare response payloads in real time. Methodically investigated every discrepancy, uncovered subtle legacy edge-case bugs, and mitigated them in code until reaching 100% shadow parity.
  • Geo & Vehicle Phased Rollout: Progressively rolled out live traffic by geographic regions (cities) and vehicle types (2-wheelers, 3-wheelers, commercial trucks) with automated circuit breakers and instant rollback capabilities.
Measurable Outcome

100% Zero Downtime, Zero Pricing Leakage & Decoupled Architecture

Individually orchestrated and executed the migration of ~97 combined RPS across core revenue paths with zero seconds of downtime and zero calculation discrepancies. Replaced the unmaintainable 18-table monolith database coupling with an isolated, domain-driven microservice and dedicated database, accelerating feature deployment velocity for pricing and marketing teams while securing absolute financial settlement accuracy.

02 · SDE-1: DATA INFRASTRUCTURE

End-to-End Snowflake Analytical Pipeline

Operational relational databases were facing severe IOPS saturation and disk bloat from long-running analytics queries. I designed an asynchronous streaming ingestion pipeline into Snowflake to isolate operational workloads and streamline analytics.

└─────────────────────────┘      │ Workers (Kotlin / Ruby) │

                                             │ Batch Micro-Chunks
                                             ▼
                                 ┌─────────────────────────┐
                                 │ Snowflake Data Lake     │
                                 │ • Partitioned Storage   │
                                 │ • Schema Pruned         │
                                 │ • Deduplication Engine  │
                                 └─────────────────────────┘
The Challenge

Storage Bloat & Relational Contention

  • High-volume booking and allocation transactions generated hundreds of gigabytes of historical event logs monthly.
  • Operational OLTP instances suffered CPU spikes of over 85% whenever data science and reporting teams queried raw transaction tables.
  • Index fragmentation and uncontrolled schema growth were causing exponential cloud storage cost growth.
Engineering Strategy

Streamlined Ingestion & Partitioning

  • Decoupled OLTP workloads by streaming CDC events into Kafka topics with idempotent deduplication keys.
  • Configured batch micro-chunk staging to write directly into Snowflake external tables with auto-clustering on timestamp and city partitions.
  • Implemented automated TTL and retention policies that safely pruned redundant raw payloads from operational disks.
Measurable Outcome

2.5 TB Storage Reduced & 2.35% Cluster CPU Savings

Freed up 2.5 Terabytes of primary database disk space, dropped operational cluster CPU utilization by 2.35% across the board, and lowered data query latency for analytics teams from minutes to seconds.

03 · SDE-1: BACKEND ARCHITECTURE

Fare Rule Engine: 18 Tables Reduced to 2

Porter's dynamic pricing involves numerous dimensions: vehicle categories, distance tiers, peak hours, regional tolls, demand multipliers, and custom corporate rules. The legacy implementation spread these across 18 tightly-coupled database tables.

fare_engine.sys — schema redesign 
BEFORE (Legacy Architecture):
┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐
│ BaseFare │──►│ DistTier │──►│ PeakSurg │──►│ TollRule │ (18 relational tables)
└──────────┘   └──────────┘   └──────────┘   └──────────┘  Schema migration for every change!

AFTER (Dynamic Declarative Engine):
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ fare_rules (Master Rule Config)      │     │ fare_rule_conditions (Polymorphic)   │
│ • rule_id, city, vehicle, priority   │ ──► │ • condition_type (TIME, GEO, DEMAND) │
│ • formula_type, base_amount, status  │     │ • operator, operand_json, weights    │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘     └──────────────────────────────────────┘
                   ▲
                   │ Evaluated in-memory via cached rule trees (0 DB migrations!)
┌──────────────────┴───────────────────┐
│ Booking Request: Origin, Dest, Time  │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
The Problem

Migration Friction & Query Overhead

  • Every new business pricing knob (e.g. wet-weather surcharges, special festival pricing) required writing and executing manual database migrations.
  • Executing 18-table relational joins during booking quotation introduced latency penalties on hot P0 checkout paths.
  • High risk of regression bugs when product managers requested rule adjustments.
The Solution

Declarative In-Memory Rule Evaluation

  • Consolidated the entire schema into 2 polymorphic tables: fare_rules and fare_rule_conditions.
  • Engineered a condition evaluation engine that parses JSON condition trees in-memory, cached in Redis with sub-millisecond lookups.
  • Allowed operations teams to configure new pricing levers via internal portals without requiring engineering deployments.
Business & Tech Impact

Zero Schema Migrations & Instant Pricing Agility

Completely eliminated the need for database migrations when launching new fare rules. Pricing changes that previously took days of deployment cycles can now be deployed instantly with zero downtime.

04 · SDE-1: SCALABILITY & RELIABILITY

6.5 Crore GST Invoices & Latency Optimization

A mandatory regulatory compliance update required recalculating and regenerating 6.5 crore (65 million) historical GST tax invoices across India without bringing down ongoing customer booking transactions.

The Bottleneck

Queue Choking & High p99 Latency

  • The existing invoice generation worker suffered from a 6.6s p99 latency due to unindexed lookups and unoptimized PDF generation calls.
  • Enqueuing 65,000,000 regeneration jobs threatened to starve standard real-time booking invoice queues.
  • Strict statutory compliance required 100% tax calculation accuracy with zero rounding or missing discrepancies.
Optimization Strategy

Worker Concurrency & Parallel Chunking

  • Separated regeneration workers into dedicated low-priority worker pools, safeguarding live P0 checkout queues.
  • Batch-fetched invoice records using composite indexed keys, reducing DB round-trips by over 80%.
  • Cached static state-wise GST rules and templated PDF asset memory buffers.
  • Tuned worker thread pools and connection pooling in Ruby and Kotlin services.
Results

Latency Cut from 6.6s to 0.8s (8.25x Faster)

Successfully processed and generated all 6.5 crore GST invoices ahead of regulatory deadlines with zero downtime. Slashed the invoice generation worker p99 latency from 6.6 seconds down to 0.8 seconds, saving significant compute cycles.

05 · SDE-1: COST OPTIMIZATION & AUTOMATION

Marketplace Shard Merge & OLC Retry Automation

Infrastructure Optimization

Marketplace Shard Consolidation

Customer Experience & Ops

Order Lifecycle (OLC) Retry Automation

Transient network hiccups or downstream payment webhook timeouts occasionally caused order lifecycle state machines to stall, generating manual support tickets for customer care (CC) agents.

Implemented an automated exponential-backoff retry framework with dead-letter queue (DLQ) re-processing that autonomously resolved stalled states.

Impact: Reduced customer care agent escalations related to OLC stalls to 0%.
